Open Math Progress (OMP) is an independent, community-oriented project that aggregates publicly reported developments on mathematical research problems.

Our goal is to make mathematical progress easier to discover, trace, compare, and build upon, while respecting the intellectual contributions of mathematicians.

**We record claims, not grant rights. We preserve evidence, not judge priority.**

## What We Collect

OMP aims to document:

- Publicly reported solutions and counterexamples.
- Partial progress on open mathematical problems.
- AI-assisted research results and their original sources.
- Corrections, revisions, disputes, and retractions.
- Public notices concerning prior research and scholarly contributions.

Records are linked to their original sources. We do not seek to replace the repositories or publications in which the research first appeared.

Coverage is incomplete and depends on the supported source formats.

## Verification and Research Status

**Inclusion in OMP does not imply mathematical verification.**

Statements such as *solved*, *proved*, or *refuted* are treated as claims reported by their original sources unless independent evidence is explicitly documented.

OMP does not independently verify mathematical correctness, novelty, completeness, or academic priority.

An automated indexing process is not a mathematical proof review.

## Respect for Mathematicians

We recognize the intellectual contributions of mathematicians, including problem formulation, partial results, theoretical methods, independent verification, and sustained research.

Researchers who believe that their prior or parallel work may be relevant to an AI-assisted discovery are welcome to submit a **Public Research Notice**.

Notices may include earlier publications, research contributions, independent work, or attribution concerns.

Notices are publicly accessible and unreviewed. Their publication does not establish ownership, scientific priority, authorship, or misconduct.

Researchers are not required to disclose confidential or unpublished materials.

## Automation and Transparency

OMP uses GitHub Actions and source-specific adapters to collect publicly accessible research records.

The index is generated from structured metadata and source links.

The automated system does not determine whether a mathematical proof is correct.

For more information, see [Methodology](METHODOLOGY.md).

## Contributing

You can contribute by suggesting new research sources, submitting public progress reports, documenting relevant prior work, or identifying incorrect metadata and broken links.

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [RESEARCH_ETHICS.md](RESEARCH_ETHICS.md) before contributing.

## Independence and Attribution

OMP is an independent aggregation project and is not affiliated with the organizations or researchers whose work it indexes.

Original research, manuscripts, code, and associated intellectual contributions remain attributed to their respective sources and contributors.

The repository does not certify mathematical solutions or adjudicate scholarly disputes.
