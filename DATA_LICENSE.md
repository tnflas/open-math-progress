# Data and Licensing

## 1. General Principle

OMP does not claim ownership of mathematical discoveries indexed from external sources.

Publication of an index entry does not transfer third-party copyright or other intellectual property rights.

## 2. Project-Generated Metadata

OMP intends to make metadata independently created by its maintainers freely reusable.

To the extent OMP owns applicable rights and expressly applies CC0, such material may be released under CC0 1.0:

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This statement does not relicense third-party material.

## 3. Software

Project software is governed by the repository's applicable software license, including the root LICENSE where relevant.

Check individual files for additional notices.

## 4. External Sources

Third-party research descriptions, reports, databases, figures, code, and other protected materials retain their respective rights and licenses.

Some sources may require attribution, license preservation, or other conditions.

The presence of a link or record in OMP does not imply that its original content is CC0 or MIT licensed.

## 5. Research Notices and Community Submissions

Contributors retain applicable rights to material they submit, subject to GitHub's terms and any licenses explicitly granted.

Public submission does not transfer scientific priority or ownership to OMP.

Do not submit unauthorized confidential or copyrighted material.

## 6. Reuse

Users should follow original links and inspect relevant source licenses before reproducing substantial text, datasets, figures, or code.

Mathematical facts and source attribution should not be confused with copyright licensing of their original expression.

This document is a general policy statement, not legal advice.
