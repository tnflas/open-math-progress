# Submission Guide

## Submit a Public Research Notice

1. Open the repository's **Discussions** tab.
2. Choose **Research Notices**.
3. Identify the relevant problem or existing source record.
4. Describe your prior research, parallel work, or attribution concern.
5. Include public references when appropriate.
6. Publish only material you are willing to disclose.

The notice becomes publicly accessible.

The repository does not verify identity, mathematical correctness, or academic priority.

## Submit a Research Result

1. Open **Discussions**.
2. Choose **Solution & Progress Reports**.
3. Provide the precise problem or variant.
4. Link to the original public report.
5. Describe what the source claims.
6. State known AI involvement and verification evidence if available.

Submission does not guarantee automatic indexing.

## Request a Correction

1. Open **Corrections & Responses**.
2. Link to the inaccurate record.
3. Explain the requested change.
4. Add public supporting references.

## Protect Unpublished Work

Do not publish unpublished proofs, private correspondence, or confidential information unless you are authorized and willing to make them public.

An earlier research start date does not, by itself, establish exclusive rights to subsequent independent discoveries.
