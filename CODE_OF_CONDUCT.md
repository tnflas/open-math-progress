# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

Participants should discuss research and attribution concerns respectfully and in good faith.

Disagreement about mathematical proofs, sources, or research history is permitted.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Prohibited conduct includes:

- Harassment and threats.
- Impersonation.
- Publication of private information without authorization.
- Spam or coordinated disruption.
- Knowingly misleading attribution.
- Abusive or targeted personal accusations.
- Unauthorized distribution of confidential material.

## Academic Disputes

OMP does not decide mathematical correctness or scientific priority.

Public disagreement does not automatically constitute misconduct.

Discussions should distinguish documented facts, source-reported statements, and personal interpretations.

## Reporting Problems

Use GitHub's reporting and moderation mechanisms for abusive content.

For non-sensitive metadata corrections, use Corrections & Responses or Issues.

Avoid publishing personal or confidential information in a public complaint.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate content to protect users and comply with applicable laws and GitHub policies.

Moderation decisions do not certify or reject mathematical claims.
