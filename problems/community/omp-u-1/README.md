# Ryser's Conjecture

**Problem ID:** `OMP-U-1`

**Mathematical verification:** Not assessed.

## Statement

**Ryser's Conjecture (Latin Square Transversals)**

A Latin square of order \(n\) is an \(n\times n\) array filled with \(n\) symbols, each appearing exactly once in every row and every column.

A *transversal* is a selection of \(n\) cells such that no two selected cells share a row, a column, or a symbol.

**Ryser's conjecture** asserts that every Latin square of odd order has a transversal.

Equivalently, every proper edge-coloring of the complete bipartite graph \(K_{n,n}\) with \(n\) colors contains a rainbow perfect matching.

The conjecture remains open in general. It is closely related to the Brualdi–Stein conjecture, which asserts that every Latin square of order \(n\) has a partial transversal of size at least \(n-1\).
