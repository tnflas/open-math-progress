# Two Integer-Weight Questions of Pula: An Abelian Quotient Criterion for Latin Squares

## Abstract

This research note studies two integer-weight questions posed by Kyle Pula concerning Latin squares. Using an integral signed-incidence presentation and the universal Abelian quotient of a loop, it presents candidate affirmative answers to both questions: a characterization of the absence of odd integer weights through odd-block cyclic-group patterns, and the existence of integer near-1-weights for every Latin square. The paper also establishes an exact integer-margin feasibility criterion and derives further consequences for omitted triples and maximality.

The manuscript was developed with substantial generative-AI assistance. It has not undergone independent peer review, and no claim of novelty or academic priority is made. Its purpose is to facilitate mathematical verification and discussion.
