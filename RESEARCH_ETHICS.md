# Research Ethics and Academic Credit

## 1. Mission

Open Math Progress supports transparent discovery and documentation of mathematical research progress.

We recognize the intellectual contributions of mathematicians, including problem formulation, sustained research, partial results, new methods, proofs, and independent verification.

Our purpose is not to appropriate or diminish these contributions.

## 2. No Scientific Adjudication

OMP does not independently establish mathematical correctness, originality, authorship, scientific priority, or ownership.

The appearance of a result in this repository does not confer exclusive research rights.

A publicly submitted notice does not establish that another researcher acted improperly.

## 3. Public Research Notices

Researchers may publish notices concerning:

- Prior published work.
- Independent or parallel research.
- Relevant partial results.
- Attribution or acknowledgment concerns.
- Corrections and responses.

These are contributor-submitted, unreviewed statements.

The repository provides public visibility, not a legal or academic determination.

Research on a problem that began earlier does not, by itself, create exclusive rights to later independent solutions.

## 4. Right to Respond

Researchers may submit public responses or additional references.

Where technically possible, related statements may be linked.

OMP does not decide which account is correct solely by displaying its submission.

## 5. AI-Assisted Research

AI-assisted results are welcome.

Where supported by source records, AI involvement may be documented.

Researchers remain responsible for their own scholarly representations, citations, and submissions.

AI assistance neither automatically erases human contributions nor automatically establishes a valid discovery claim.

## 6. Attribution

OMP aims to preserve original source links and available attribution.

The index does not claim ownership over third-party mathematical research.

Public inclusion is not a transfer of intellectual property rights.

## 7. Privacy and Confidentiality

Do not disclose:

- Confidential or unpublished third-party research.
- Private correspondence without authorization.
- Sensitive personal information.
- Credentials or private account information.

Contributors should disclose only material they are authorized and willing to publish.

## 8. Moderation

The maintainers do not review mathematical correctness or adjudicate scientific disputes.

They may restrict content involving spam, impersonation, threats, harassment, privacy violations, or other violations of law or platform rules.

Content moderation does not constitute mathematical or legal adjudication.

## 9. Corrections

Participants may submit documented corrections and responses.

The original record and subsequent changes may be retained for transparency, subject to privacy, legal, and platform requirements.

**We record claims, not grant rights. We preserve evidence, not judge priority.**
