# Research Ethics

We respect mathematicians' intellectual contributions,
including prior, parallel, and ongoing research.

Researchers may publish Research Notices documenting relevant
contributions, earlier results, or attribution concerns.

Notices are public, unreviewed statements. Their publication
does not establish ownership, authorship, misconduct, or priority.

The repository does not adjudicate academic disputes.

Do not disclose confidential research, private correspondence,
or third-party personal information.

**We record claims, not grant rights.
We preserve evidence, not judge priority.**
